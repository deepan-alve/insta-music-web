No sound?

  1. Click the page once. Browsers hold back sound until you click. The button on the photo pulses until then.
  2. Check the switch. Open Postsound from the toolbar and turn on “Play songs on photo posts”.
  3. In Firefox, click the play icon in the address bar and allow audio for instagram.com.
Postsound Collects nothing. Saves only your sound settings, in your browser. Not affiliated with Instagram or Meta.
