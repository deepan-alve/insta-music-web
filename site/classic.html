Instagram photos, with their music.

In the app, photo and carousel posts play their song. On instagram.com they stay silent. Postsound plays them in your browser, from the part of the song the poster picked.

Why Instagram on the web has no music

The Instagram app plays a photo post’s song from the post’s music data. The website loads the same posts without that data, so the photo shows and the song never starts.

Postsound asks instagram.com for each post’s song, the way the site asks for any post detail, and plays the clip the poster chose. Reels already have sound, so it leaves them alone.

It behaves like the app

  • One song at a time. The post you are looking at plays. Scroll on and the next one takes over.
  • Everywhere you browse. The home feed, profiles, post pages and the post popup.
  • Stays out of the way. It stops when you scroll past, close the post or switch tabs, and goes quiet when a video plays with sound.
  • Your volume, your call. Mute any photo, set the volume, or switch it off from the toolbar.

Questions

Is Postsound free?

Yes. It has no paid tier, no ads and no account. If you want to support it, you can buy me a sticker.

What data does it collect?

None. It has no servers. It asks instagram.com for the song of the post on your screen, and saves only your sound settings in your browser. Read the privacy policy.

Why is there no sound when I open Instagram?

Browsers hold back sound until you click the page once. The button on the photo pulses until then. In Firefox you can also allow audio for instagram.com from the play icon in the address bar.

Does it work on Reels?

Reels already play their own sound, so Postsound skips them. It works on photo and carousel posts that have a song.

Does it work on my phone?

The Instagram app already plays photo music. Postsound is for desktop browsers. The Firefox version also runs on Firefox for Android.

Is it made by Instagram?

No. Postsound is an independent extension and is not affiliated with Instagram or Meta.
