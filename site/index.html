How it finds the song

  1. 01You land on a photo post.In the feed, on a profile, or in the post popup.
  2. 02Postsound asks instagram.com for its song.The same post details the app already gets.
  3. 03It plays the part the poster picked.Scroll on and the next post takes over.
0bytes collectedNo servers, no analytics. Only your sound settings, saved in your browser.
1song at a timeIt stops when you scroll past, close the post or switch tabs.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. No paid tier, no ads, no account.

Why no sound when I open Instagram?

Browsers hold sound until you click the page once. The button on the photo pulses until then.

Does it work on Reels?

Reels already play their own sound, so Postsound skips them.

Is it made by Instagram?

No. Postsound is independent and not affiliated with Instagram or Meta.

Something broke?

Open the popup and pick Report a problem, or write to contact@deepanalve.dev.

What data does it collect?

None. Read the privacy policy.
