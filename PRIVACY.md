# Postsound privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Postsound is a browser extension that plays the music attached to Instagram photo and carousel posts on instagram.com.

## What Postsound collects

Nothing. Postsound has no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no tracking.

## What Postsound does with data

- When an Instagram post is on your screen, Postsound asks instagram.com for that post's details, including its song. The request goes straight from your browser to instagram.com with your own Instagram session, the same way the Instagram website loads post details.
- Postsound plays the song's audio file from Instagram's servers in your browser.
- Postsound saves one setting in your browser's extension storage: whether you turned the sound on or off.

Postsound does not send any data to its developer or to any third party. It does not sell, share or transfer data.
